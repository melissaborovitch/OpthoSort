📤 Step 1: Upload Your Photos
📱 Works on Phone! Upload photos from your camera roll or take new photos directly. Photos are grouped into patients automatically by when they were taken — each burst of photos (name chart + eye photos) becomes one patient folder. All processing stays on your device.

🗂️ Step 3: Organize & Name
Name each patient folder:
• Double-click any photo (or tap 🔍) to zoom in and read a name screenshot
• Click a folder title and type the patient's name
• Same patient, later visit? Type the same name — the app will offer to merge the folders (or use 🔗 Merge)
• On desktop, drag photos between folders to fix grouping
• The "Unsorted" folder holds anything that couldn't be grouped
